    Trackport · MP3 file renderer

    Using Trackport

    Export a full recording

    Add local files or paste a direct HTTPS audio download. Select a bitrate, render the queue, then save an MP3 or a ZIP. The ZIP contains ordinary MP3 files you can extract and copy to another device.

    Use a music-service link

    Paste a shared song link or Spotify track URI. Apple Music and Spotify supply track details. Attach your copy of the recording, such as a downloaded iTunes Store purchase or an artist-provided file. Subscription offline downloads play inside their respective apps.

    Formats and storage

    New encodes use mono or stereo at 44.1 kHz. Existing MP3s copy exactly. Format decoding depends on your browser. Full audio stays on this computer. Download URLs go to their hosts, and catalog lookups contact the selected service. The import limit is 256 MiB per file. Very long decodes need additional memory.

    Closing this tab discards the queue. Save your exports first, then choose End session or close the Trackport console.
